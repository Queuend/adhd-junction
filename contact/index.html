CONTACT ADHD JUNCTION

Write to us.

Questions, corrections and suggestions are welcome. Tell us just enough to help us understand what you need.

BY EMAIL

Get in touch.

ADHD Junction does not use an online contact form or store messages on this website.

hello@adhdjunction.org→

Use this address for corrections, questions about our sources, suggestions, privacy enquiries or accessibility enquiries.

Please don’t send medical records or detailed sensitive health information by email. ADHD Junction cannot provide emergency help, diagnosis, treatment or individual medical care.

We read corrections carefully. We may not be able to answer every general enquiry, and contacting us does not create a clinician–patient relationship.
